## The Problem

Standard voice interfaces and virtual assistants struggle to understand native Assamese accents, phonetic nuances, and regional dialects.

## The Idea

Fine-tune open-source models like OpenAI Whisper or Wav2Vec2 on crowdsourced Assamese voice recordings to create a lightweight, open-source Speech-to-Text model that can run efficiently on everyday smartphones and computers.

## Key Goals

- **Regional Accent Support:** Accommodate diverse spoken styles across Lower, Central, and Upper Assam.
- **Offline & On-Device Capability:** Allow lightweight local inference without relying on constant cloud connectivity.
- **Open-Source Weights & Benchmarks:** Release model checkpoints and evaluation sets publicly for community research.

## Status & Roadmap

> **Community Proposal — In Planning**
> This is an open community initiative. Audio contributors, linguists, and machine learning engineers are currently being onboarded. Benchmark datasets and prototype demos will be released as progress develops.
