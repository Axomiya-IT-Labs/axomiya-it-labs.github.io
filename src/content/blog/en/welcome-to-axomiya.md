## Welcome to Axomiya IT Labs

We are excited to kick off our open-source journey with you. **Axomiya IT Labs** is a community-driven initiative focused on building artificial intelligence applications, educational tools, and open developer resources.

## Our Mission

We aim to:
- Democratize AI education and practical tools across languages and regions
- Build open-source software libraries, datasets, and frameworks rooted in Assam
- Foster collaborative mentorship for students, developers, and educators
- Enable digital opportunity and technical understanding for curious learners everywhere

## Get Involved

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and skill levels. Whether you are an experienced software engineer, a designer, a writer, a translator, or someone taking their first steps in tech, there is a place for you in our community.

Connect with us on our Telegram channel, explore our initiatives on the website, and contribute on GitHub.

Stay tuned for more updates, tutorials, and project releases.
